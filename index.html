About the Island

A Jewel of the Aegean Sea

Santorini is one of the most stunning islands of the Cyclades, rising dramatically from the sea with its iconic caldera formed by one of the largest volcanic eruptions in history.

Known for its unique volcanic landscape, dazzling white architecture, and spectacular sea views, it attracts travellers seeking both beauty and tranquility.

  • Best time to visit:  Late spring and early autumn for pleasant weather and fewer crowds
  • Famous for:  Sunsets in Oia, black & red beaches, and local Assyrtiko wine
  • Transportation:  Ferry from Athens (Piraeus) or direct flights to Santorini Airport

Places to Visit

Top Attractions

Oia Village

The most iconic spot on the island — stunning sunsets, cave houses, and breathtaking caldera views.

Red Beach

A dramatic volcanic beach surrounded by towering crimson cliffs and crystal-clear turquoise water.

Fira Town

The vibrant capital of Santorini, packed with cafés, boutiques, and sweeping caldera panoramas.
